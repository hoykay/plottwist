# PlotTwist

One line: Compare the job someone applied for with the final offer they received, then show what changed, what is missing, and what they may want to clarify before signing.

## The Unique Kernel

PlotTwist is not a general career assistant.

Its core idea is to compare the original job posting with the final offer and make important differences impossible to overlook.

The memorable moment is seeing terms such as salary, work arrangement, PTO, bonus, title, location, benefits, working hours, or probation clearly marked as changed, missing, new, or matching, with evidence from the original documents.

## Who It's For

Job seekers who have reached the offer stage and want to make sure the role they are about to accept still matches the role they originally applied for.

Today, they usually reread the job posting and offer manually, compare details from memory, or ask friends and family for a second opinion.

## The Core Loop

The user brings in the original job posting and the final job offer.

PlotTwist compares the important employment terms, shows what changed or was omitted, links those findings back to the source text, and reminds the user what may be worth clarifying before signing.

The product becomes useful again whenever the user receives another job offer.

## Inspiration & Identity

PlotTwist should feel like a real, polished consumer product rather than a hackathon prototype.

The experience should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Trustworthy
- Easy to understand
- Smooth and uncluttered
- Intentionally designed

Avoid a generic AI-app or strong “vibe-coded” appearance.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The learner wants to build something independently that people genuinely want to use.

The goal is to create a useful product that helps job seekers protect themselves, feels strong enough to share with friends and family, and demonstrates that a polished product can be built from scratch through to completion.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A judge can complete the full experience smoothly from start to finish and feel like PlotTwist is a real product rather than something built only for a hackathon.

The key moment is when the comparison reveals something important the user might otherwise have missed, such as:

- a lower salary,
- a remote role becoming hybrid,
- a promised bonus disappearing,
- fewer PTO days,
- or a new probation period.

The result should clearly show the difference and help the user understand what they may want to clarify before signing.

## The POC Boundary

The proof of concept focuses only on the core comparison experience:

- Provide the original job posting
- Provide the final job offer
- Compare important employment terms
- Classify findings as matching, changed, missing, or new
- Show source-linked evidence
- Surface important items the user may want to clarify
- Deliver the result through a polished, smooth interface

## Later

Potential future additions include:

- Saved comparison history
- Accounts and user profiles
- Shareable or exportable reports
- Salary benchmarking
- Job application tracking
- Browser extensions
- Integrations with job platforms or email
- More advanced career tools

## Explicitly Cut

- **Accounts and login** — not needed to prove the core experience.
- **Job application tracker** — would make PlotTwist broader and less focused.
- **Resume tools** — unrelated to the core comparison problem.
- **Interview preparation** — useful elsewhere, but not part of the product kernel.
- **Negotiation chatbot** — risks turning PlotTwist into a generic AI career assistant.
- **Salary market benchmarking** — adds complexity without improving the main demo.
- **Recruiter or employer tools** — the first version is built for job seekers.
- **Mobile app** — a responsive web experience is enough for the proof of concept.
- **Legal judgments** — PlotTwist highlights differences and questions to clarify, but does not decide whether employment terms are lawful.
- **Complex integrations** — unnecessary for proving the core idea.
